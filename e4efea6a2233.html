Sheet-Pan Lemon-Garlic Chicken Thighs & Veggies

~600 kcal per serving · 5 min prep · 30 min oven · 220 °C / 425 °F
Author Recipe Bridge

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 800 g bone-in skin-on chicken thighs (≈4 large)
  • 800 g baby potatoes, halved
  • 400 g broccoli florets
  • 300 g carrots, cut 2 cm pieces
  • 45 ml olive oil
  • 15 g garlic, minced (≈5 cloves)
  • 10 g dried oregano
  • 10 g dried thyme
  • Zest + juice of 1 lemon (≈30 ml juice)
  • Salt & pepper

Instructions

  • Heat oven 220 °C / 425 °F. Line large sheet pan with foil.
  • Toss potatoes, carrots, broccoli with 30 ml oil, half the garlic, oregano, thyme, salt, pepper. Spread on pan.
  • Pat thighs dry; place on veg. Brush with remaining oil, garlic, lemon zest, salt, pepper.
  • Roast 25-30 min until thighs hit 82 °C and veg are tender.
  • Squeeze lemon juice over everything. Divide 4 ways.
Servings 4
