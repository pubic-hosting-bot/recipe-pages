Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry

For extra crispiness, pat chicken dry before cooking. Adjust vegetable quantities based on preference. Leftovers store well in refrigerator for 3-4 days and reheat well in microwave or stove.
Author Recipe Bridge

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 150 g broccoli florets
  • 100 g broccoli stems, sliced
  • 100 g carrots, julienned
  • 100 g red bell pepper, sliced
  • 100 g yellow bell pepper, sliced
  • 100 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced (20 g)
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced (15 g)
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil (30 ml)
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil (15 ml)
  • 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce (45 ml)
  • 1 tbsp honey (21 g)
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds (5 g)
  • 2 g reen onions, sliced (for garnish)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  • Prepare all ingredients: cut chicken into bite-sized pieces, chop vegetables, mince garlic and ginger.
  • In a small bowl, mix soy sauce, honey, and sesame oil for the sauce.
  • Heat 1 tbsp vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat.
  • Add chicken pieces, season with salt and pepper, and stir-fry for 5-6 minutes until cooked through and lightly browned. Remove chicken from pan and set aside.
  • Add remaining 1 tbsp vegetable oil to the same pan.
  • Add onion, garlic, and ginger; stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  • Add harder vegetables first (carrots, broccoli stems) and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  • Add softer vegetables (broccoli florets, bell peppers, mushrooms) and stir-fry for another 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender.
  • Return chicken to the pan with vegetables.
  • Pour the sauce over the chicken and vegetables, tossing to coat evenly.
  • Cook for another 1-2 minutes until sauce thickens slightly and everything is heated through.
  • Remove from heat, drizzle with sesame oil, sprinkle with sesame seeds and green onions.
  • Serve immediately over cooked brown rice, quinoa, or cauliflower rice if desired.
Servings 4
