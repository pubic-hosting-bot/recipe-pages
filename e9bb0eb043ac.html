Sheet Pan Honey Garlic Chicken and Vegetables

For a complete meal, serve with cooked quinoa, brown rice, or couscous. Leftovers store well in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and can be reheated in the microwave or oven.
Author Recipe Bridge

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breasts, boneless, skinless, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 300 g baby carrots, halved lengthwise
  • 400 g broccoli florets
  • 200 g snow peas, trimmed
  • 150 g red bell pepper, sliced into strips
  • 30 ml olive oil
  • 45 ml honey
  • 15 ml low-sodium soy sauce
  • 15 ml Dijon mustard
  • 12 g garlic, minced (about 4 cloves)
  • 5 g sesame seeds
  • 2 g salt
  • 1 g black pepper
  • 5 ml lemon juice (optional)

Instructions

  • Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a large sheet pan with parchment paper.
  • In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, honey, soy sauce, Dijon mustard, minced garlic, salt, pepper, and lemon juice (if using).
  • Place chicken breasts, baby carrots, broccoli florets, snow peas, and bell pepper on the prepared sheet pan.
  • Pour the honey garlic sauce over the chicken and vegetables; toss to coat evenly.
  • Spread in an even layer, ensuring chicken is in a single layer.
  • Bake for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway through, until chicken is cooked through (internal temperature 75°C) and vegetables are tender-crisp.
  • Remove from oven, sprinkle with sesame seeds, and serve immediately.
Servings 4
