Spicy Salmon Poke Bowl

Author Recipe Bridge

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 300 g sushi rice
  • 360 ml water
  • 600 g sushi-grade salmon, skinless, diced into 1.5-cm cubes
  • 60 ml low-sodium soy sauce
  • 30 ml rice vinegar
  • 15 ml sesame oil
  • 15 ml sriracha
  • 15 ml honey
  • 15 ml fresh lime juice
  • 5 g fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 g garlic powder
  • 10 g toasted sesame seeds
  • 300 g cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 150 g carrot, grated
  • 180 g shelled edamame, cooked
  • 300 g avocado, sliced (about 2 medium)
  • 30 g green onions, sliced thinly
  • 30 g pickled ginger (optional)
  • 10 g nori strips (optional)

Instructions

  • Rinse the sushi rice under cold water until water runs clear. Combine rice and water in a saucepan, bring to a boil, then reduce to low, cover and simmer 12-15 minutes until water is absorbed. Remove from heat, let stand 10 minutes, then fluff and let cool to room temperature.
  • In a bowl whisk together soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, sriracha, honey, lime juice, grated ginger, garlic powder and sesame seeds to make the poke sauce.
  • Add the diced salmon to the sauce, toss to coat, and let marinate for 10-15 minutes at room temperature (or refrigerate up to 30 minutes).
  • While the salmon marinates, prepare the toppings: slice cucumber, grate carrot, shell edamame, slice avocado and green onions, and prepare pickled ginger and nori if using.
  • To assemble each bowl, place a bed of cooled rice (about 150 g per bowl), top with the marinated salmon and its sauce, then arrange the cucumber, carrot, edamame, avocado, green onions, pickled ginger and nori around the bowl.
  • Serve immediately, with extra sauce on the side if desired.
Servings 6
