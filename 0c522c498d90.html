15-Min Sesame-Ginger Tofu & Green Bean Stir-Fry

~590 kcal per serving · 5 min prep · 10 min cook · vegetarian
Author Recipe Bridge

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 450 g firm tofu, pressed, 2 cm cubes
  • 500 g green beans, trimmed
  • 200 g snap peas
  • 30 ml vegetable oil (divided)
  • 20 g garlic, minced
  • 10 g ginger, grated
  • 45 ml soy sauce
  • 30 ml water
  • 15 ml rice vinegar
  • 15 g honey
  • 15 ml sesame oil
  • 10 g cornstarch
  • 5 g chili flakes (optional)
  • 400 g cooked jasmine rice (≈150 g dry)
  • 20 g sesame seeds
  • 30 g green onions, sliced

Instructions

  • Cook rice per package (or use leftover/day-old).
  • Heat 15 ml oil in large wok/skillet over high. Sear tofu 3 min until golden; remove.
  • Add 15 ml oil, green beans, snap peas; stir-fry 3 min.
  • Add garlic, ginger; 30 s.
  • Return tofu, pour sauce; toss 1 min until thickened.
  • Divide rice into 4, top stir-fry, sprinkle seeds & onions.
Servings 4
