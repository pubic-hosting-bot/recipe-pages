Mediterranean Chickpea & Roasted Vegetable Bowls with Tahini Dressing

Vegan • Meal-prep friendly • No lemon, chili, lentils, or quinoa • ~620 kcal per serving • Prep: 20 min • Cook: 35 min • Reheats beautifully
Author Recipe Bridge

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 300 g brown rice (dry weight)
  • 500 g canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed (≈3 cups)
  • 300 g red bell peppers, cut into 2 cm strips (≈2 large)
  • 200 g zucchini, cut into 1.5 cm half-moons
  • 150 g red onion, cut into wedges
  • 200 g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 25 ml olive oil (for roasting vegetables)
  • 5 g smoked paprika
  • 3 g ground cumin
  • 2 g dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 50 g tahini
  • 25 ml olive oil (for dressing)
  • 60 ml warm water
  • 10 g apple cider vinegar
  • 5 g garlic, minced (≈1.5 cloves)
  • 3 g ground cumin
  • 2 g dried oregano
  • Salt to taste
  • 30 g pine nuts, toasted
  • 50 g Kalamata olives, pitted and halved
  • 20 g fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

  • Cook rice: Rinse 300 g brown rice. Cook in 600 ml water with pinch of salt for 25-30 min until tender. Drain if needed. Fluff and cool.
  • Roast vegetables: Preheat oven to 220 °C / 425 °F. Line large sheet pan with parchment. Toss bell peppers, zucchini, red onion with 25 ml olive oil, smoked paprika, 3 g cumin, oregano, salt, pepper. Spread evenly. Roast 20 min. Add cherry tomatoes; roast 10 min more until vegetables are tender and lightly charred.
  • Crisp chickpeas: Pat 500 g chickpeas dry. Toss with 1 tsp olive oil, pinch of salt. Spread on separate small sheet pan. Roast alongside vegetables for last 15 min until crisp.
  • Make dressing: Whisk tahini, 25 ml olive oil, warm water, apple cider vinegar, minced garlic, 3 g cumin, oregano, salt until smooth. Add 1-2 Tbsp more water if too thick.
  • Toast pine nuts: Dry-toast in skillet over medium heat 2-3 min until golden; watch closely.
  • Assemble bowls: Divide rice, roasted vegetables, crispy chickpeas among 4 containers. Drizzle with tahini dressing. Top with toasted pine nuts, olives, and parsley.
  • Store: Keep dressing separate if prepping >1 day ahead. Reheat rice, vegetables, and chickpeas in microwave 2-3 min; add dressing after reheating.
Servings 4
