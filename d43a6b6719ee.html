Dairy-Free Tuscan Chicken with Jasmine Rice & Roasted Broccoli

Omnivore • ~575 kcal per serving • 4 servings • Prep: 15 min • Cook: 25 min • No dairy, no mushrooms, no brown rice, no chili, no lemon herb • Reheats well
Author Recipe Bridge

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless skinless chicken thighs (or breasts), cut into 2.5 cm pieces
  • 30 ml avocado oil (or olive oil), divided
  • 1 small shallot (≈50 g), finely diced
  • 4 cloves garlic (≈12 g), minced
  • 5 g Italian seasoning blend (oregano, basil, marjoram, rosemary, thyme)
  • 5 g sea salt
  • 2 g black pepper
  • 60 ml low-sodium chicken broth
  • 15 g arrowroot starch (or cornstarch)
  • 360 ml full-fat coconut milk (canned, shaken)
  • 70 g oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, drained and chopped (reserve 30 ml oil)
  • 30 g fresh basil, chopped (or 5 g dried basil)
  • 120 g fresh baby spinach
  • 15 g fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)
  • 300 g jasmine rice (dry weight)
  • 360 ml water
  • 2 g salt
  • 400 g broccoli florets (≈1 large head)
  • 15 ml olive oil
  • 2 g salt
  • 1 g black pepper
  • 5 g garlic powder (optional)

Instructions

  • Cook jasmine rice: Rinse 300 g jasmine rice in cold water until water runs clear. Drain. Combine rice, 360 ml water, and 2 g salt in saucepan. Bring to boil, reduce to low, cover, simmer 12-15 min until liquid absorbed. Remove from heat; let stand covered 5 min. Fluff with fork.
  • Roast broccoli: Preheat oven to 220 °C / 425 °F. Line sheet pan with parchment. Toss broccoli florets with 15 ml olive oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Spread in single layer. Roast 18-20 min until tender-crisp and lightly charred at edges.
  • Season chicken: Pat chicken pieces dry. Toss with 15 ml avocado oil, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
  • Sear chicken: Heat remaining 15 ml avocado oil (plus 30 ml reserved sun-dried tomato oil) in large 30 cm skillet over medium-high. Add chicken in single layer; cook 3-4 min undisturbed until golden. Flip; cook 2-3 min more until cooked through (74 °C / 165 °F). Transfer to plate.
  • Make sauce base: Reduce heat to medium. Add shallot to skillet; cook 2 min until softened. Add garlic; cook 30 s until fragrant.
  • Thicken sauce: Whisk chicken broth and arrowroot starch in small bowl until smooth. Pour into skillet; cook 1 min until slightly thickened.
  • Add coconut milk & tomatoes: Stir in coconut milk and chopped sun-dried tomatoes. Simmer 3-4 min until sauce reduces slightly.
  • Finish: Return chicken (with any juices) to skillet. Add spinach and basil; stir until spinach wilts, 1-2 min. Taste; adjust salt/pepper.
  • Plate: Divide jasmine rice (≈165 g cooked per serving), roasted broccoli (≈100 g), and Tuscan chicken with sauce among 4 containers. Garnish with parsley.
  • Store & reheat: Refrigerate up to 4 days. Microwave 2-3 min; add splash of water or broth if sauce thickens too much.
Servings 4
