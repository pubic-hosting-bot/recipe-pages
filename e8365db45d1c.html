Tofu and Vegetable Stir-Fry

Pressing tofu is crucial for getting crispy texture. For extra crispiness, toss pressed tofu with 1 tbsp cornstarch before cooking. Leftovers store well in refrigerator for 3-4 days and reheat well in microwave or stove.
Author Recipe Bridge

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 400 g extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut into bite-sized cubes
  • 150 g broccoli florets
  • 150 g red bell pepper, sliced
  • 100 g carrots, sliced
  • 20 g scallions (green onions), sliced
  • 225 g green beans, trimmed
  • 15 g garlic, minced (about 3 cloves)
  • 10 g fresh ginger, grated
  • 45 ml olive oil, divided
  • 60 ml water
  • 30 ml reduced-sodium soy sauce
  • 25 g packed light brown sugar
  • 10 g cornstarch
  • 10 ml toasted sesame oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  • Press tofu: Wrap tofu block in clean kitchen towel or paper towels, place a heavy pan or can on top, and press for 15-20 minutes to remove excess water. Cut into bite-sized cubes.
  • Prepare vegetables: cut broccoli into florets, slice bell pepper and carrots, trim green beans, slice scallions, mince garlic, grate ginger.
  • Make sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together water, soy sauce, brown sugar, and cornstarch until smooth. Set aside.
  • Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Add tofu in a single layer and cook undisturbed for 2-3 minutes until golden brown on bottom. Flip and cook another 2-3 minutes until all sides are golden. Remove tofu from pan and set aside.
  • Add remaining 2 tbsp olive oil to same pan. Add garlic and ginger, stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  • Add harder vegetables first (carrots, green beans) and stir-fry for 2-3 minutes.
  • Add softer vegetables (broccoli, bell pepper) and stir-fry for another 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender.
  • Return tofu to pan with vegetables.
  • Pour sauce over tofu and vegetables, stirring to coat evenly.
  • Cook for 1-2 minutes until sauce thickens and coats the ingredients.
  • Remove from heat, drizzle with toasted sesame oil, season with salt and pepper to taste, and toss with sliced scallions.
  • Serve immediately over brown rice, quinoa, or cauliflower rice if desired.
Servings 4
