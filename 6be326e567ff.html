One-Pot Turkish Red Lentil-Free Chili (Ground Beef)

~600 kcal per serving. No lentils - uses extra-lean beef + beans for body. Turkish-inspired chili with kidney and black beans.
Author Recipe Bridge

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 500 g 93% lean ground beef
  • 1 l arge onion (150 g), diced
  • 20 g garlic, minced (≈6 cloves)
  • 30 g tomato paste
  • 15 g chili powder
  • 5 g smoked paprika
  • 3 g cumin
  • 400 g canned diced tomatoes
  • 400 g canned kidney beans, drained
  • 400 g canned black beans, drained
  • 300 ml low-sodium beef broth
  • 15 ml olive oil
  • Salt & pepper
  • Optional garnish: 30 g cheddar, 20 g cilantro

Instructions

  • Heat oil in large pot over medium-high. Add beef; brown 5 min, breaking up.
  • Add onion, garlic; cook 2 min.
  • Stir in tomato paste, chili powder, paprika, cumin; 30 s.
  • Add tomatoes, both beans, broth. Bring to boil, reduce to simmer 15 min.
  • Season. Divide 4 ways; top with cheese/cilantro if using.
Servings 4
